## Purpose and founder context

Help Jordan identify a durable family enterprise rooted in Chesterfield County,
Virginia, with relevant regional and national connections. Useful contribution,
dependable earnings, transferable capability, ownership and local reputation all
matter. The long-term aim is a family institution that helps the community adapt
to technological change and provides future generations a dependable home base
while preserving their freedom to choose other paths.

Jordan is a full-time software engineer and primary family breadwinner, with
experience in cloud-hosted applications and internal enterprise product management.
He has three children and expects a fourth in March 2027. Preserve the benefits of
employment while investigating opportunities; account for family capacity.
Available hours, household financial baseline, capital at risk, downside limits,
required business income and desired family participation remain unknown. Do not
invent thresholds or presume unpaid family labor.

## Research and assessment criteria

Investigate recurring responsibilities: who needs an outcome, what triggers the
work, who is accountable, who can purchase it, what alternatives exist and what
failure costs. Compare services, operating businesses, ownership opportunities,
productive assets, partnerships and software where appropriate. No business
vehicle is the default.

Assess economics including delivery, founder time, sales, review, maintenance,
working capital and operating exposure. Examine repeatability, delegation,
transferable assets, buyer access, family fit and useful community contribution.
Treat AI progress as uncertainty to examine through competing scenarios; model
capability is not market evidence or a deterministic forecast of local outcomes.

Keep evidence gates distinct: organizational presence, observed workflow, a
documented problem, buying authority, a paid test, repeat purchase and repeatable
delivery. Public records can motivate investigation but cannot establish private
pain, willingness to switch, margins or willingness to pay. Prefer the next
bounded action likely to change a decision; a supported rejection is useful work.

## Agreed project decisions

The workflow uses existing agents with `gpt-6.1-sol` explicitly requested for both
supervisor and workers. Retain original public/private sources, catalog provenance
and selected records in SQLite, propose entities and relationships, compute
reproducible analyses, and maintain scoped findings and separately tracked hypotheses.
Start with one connected public database and a separate private store, adding
physical databases only for demonstrated boundaries or workloads.

The user-facing output is a continually maintained multi-page static HTML site:
significant findings, implications, opportunities, uncertainty and next actions
at its entry point, with links through reports and evidence to original sources.
Preserve prior reasoning and the reasons hypotheses are confirmed, discarded or
reopened. Private strategy and operational data stay outside Git. Basic recovery
must be verified before accumulating irreplaceable private notes.

Use Python + SQL, generated HTML/CSS, optional JavaScript, `uv` dependency management,
Markdown authoring and validated JSON agent handoffs. Build one useful research
path before generalizing infrastructure. [Q001](FIRST_RESEARCH_QUESTION.md) is a
public-purchasing pilot, not a county-wide ranking or a decision to start a business.
